Planning Your Trip

To help make the most of your journey, here is an essential overview of practical travel considerations, climate zones, and cultural guidelines.

Best Times to Visit

Because Vietnam spans over 1,600 kilometers from north to south, the climate varies considerably across regions:

Northern Region

Hanoi, Sapa, Halong: Best from October to April when the weather is mild, pleasant, and relatively dry.

Central Coast

Da Nang, Hoi An, Hue: Best from February to August with plenty of sunshine and warm sea breezes.

Southern Region

Ho Chi Minh City, Phu Quoc: Dry season runs from November to April with consistent tropical warmth.

Travel Etiquette & Tips

  • Respect Sacred Sites: Dress modestly (covering shoulders and knees) when entering temples and pagodas.
  • Currency: The official currency is Vietnamese Dong (VND). Carrying some cash is helpful in street markets.
  • Local Transport: Ride-hailing apps, high-speed trains, and domestic flights provide convenient connections.
  • Culinary Adventures: Don't miss trying Pho, Banh Mi, Bun Cha, and authentic Vietnamese iced milk coffee.
